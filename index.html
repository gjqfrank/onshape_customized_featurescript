Pulleys

Pulley Roller

pulley_roller.fs · round-tube-plug-with-pulley

在圆管端部生成带轮组件：端部收拢的锥形端领 + 多个带轮 + 挡边，一体化成型。 Generate a pulley assembly on a round tube end: tapered end collar + multiple pulleys + flanges, in one feature.

  • 6 种齿形：HTD 3M/5M、GT2 2M/3M/5M/8M，每个带轮独立选择6 tooth profiles: HTD 3M/5M, GT2 2M/3M/5M/8M, selectable per pulley
  • 标准外径：按 SDP/SI 规范 OD = PD − 2U 自动修正齿形缩放Standard OD: tooth profile auto-scaled so OD = PD − 2U per SDP/SI
  • 挡边两种风格：锥形（COTS 默认）或平圆柱（1mm，相邻共享）Two flange styles: conical (COTS, default) or flat 1mm cylinder (shared between adjacent pulleys)
  • 最小中心距约束：相邻带轮外端面不重叠，自动校验 CTCMin center-to-center check: adjacent pulley flange faces never overlap
  • 轴孔：1/2 六角、3/8 六角或自定义圆孔，可配置深度与偏移Axial bore: 1/2 hex, 3/8 hex, or custom circle with depth and offset

Holes

Circular Side Holes

circular_side_holes.fs · holes-on-curve-plane

在圆柱（实心或空心圆管）侧面上打多圈沿圆周均匀分布的径向孔。 Cut rings of evenly distributed radial holes on the side of a cylinder or tube.

  • 自动识别：选端面（圆或圆环均可）即得轴线、圆心与外径Auto-detect: pick an end face (disc or annulus) to derive axis, center, and outer radius
  • 最多 4 圈：每圈独立设置孔数、孔径、孔深、通孔选项Up to 4 rings: per-ring hole count, diameter, depth, and through option
  • 径向孔轴：垂直于轴线、指向轴心，孔径 = 可插入圆柱直径Radial axis: perpendicular to the cylinder axis, hole diameter = pin diameter
  • 通孔模式：勾选后直接打到圆柱轴线（空心管即径向贯穿）Through mode: cuts to the cylinder axis (radial through-hole on tubes)
